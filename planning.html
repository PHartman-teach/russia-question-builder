Russia & the Soviet Union (2017 Syllabus) — Planning Machine

Explore alternative thesis approaches, arguments and historical evidence.

1 · Choose the question you want to plan

Randomises the historical area, question formula, factor, outcome and period. Key Features are optional; some random questions will use one or two.

2 · Question to plan

Validity check: Is it grammatically clear? Chronologically accurate? Supported by syllabus content? Narrow enough for a 25-mark essay? Able to support a sustained argument?

3 · Compare three sample essay plans

Three different, defensible approaches to the question you generated. Each offers a thesis and four suggested body-paragraph arguments with precise historical examples or source leads. They are planning exemplars, not finished essays; test whether every piece of evidence answers the exact wording and time frame.

The evidence bank draws on historical examples and named primary sources. Items marked “source lead” require students to consult the original material before quoting. No quotations are fabricated.

4 · Syllabus key features

The seven key features from the NSW Modern History Stage 6 Syllabus (2017), Option F. If you select Key Features, they help focus the question. Otherwise you can generate freely across the topic. Related features are lightly indicated.

Syllabus source: Modern History Stage 6 Syllabus (2017), Russia and the Soviet Union, p. 72. This tool is designed exclusively for the 2017 syllabus; it is not a 2024-syllabus examination generator.
